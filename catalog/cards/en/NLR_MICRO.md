## Evidence and scope
NMC/graphite electrode tomography; gray and segmented volumes; reconstructed binder domain

## Recommended use
Estimate geometry descriptors and transport priors with voxel-size and segmentation uncertainty.

## Invalid inference to avoid
Do not call all phases experimentally imaged; numerically generated carbon-binder regions are model-derived.
