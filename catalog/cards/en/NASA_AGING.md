[한국어](../ko/NASA_AGING.md) | [Catalog](../../resources.json)

**ID:** `NASA_AGING` · **Priority:** P1 · **Kind:** experimental_dataset

## Evidence and scope
18650 charge/discharge/impedance; some accelerated deep discharge; source catalog fields documented

## Recommended use
Build ingestion and degradation-observable QA; compare capacity, impedance and operating conditions without mixing cells.

## Invalid inference to avoid
Not a modern EV full-pack dataset or a universal safe operating envelope. Original aggressive cutoffs are test conditions, not charging recommendations.
