## EPA schedule
Provider: **United States Environmental Protection Agency**. [US06 source](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/other-files/2025-03/us06col.txt), [schedule portal](https://www.epa.gov/vehicle-and-fuel-emissions-testing/dynamometer-drive-schedules), [copyright policy](https://www.epa.gov/web-policies-and-procedures/epa-disclaimers).

The 601-row numeric schedule was transcribed from the public plain-text source, then separately converted from mph to m/s. This is a scientific research/educational capture of a prescribed schedule, not an EPA-endorsed vehicle model. The EPA policy describes scientific/educational distribution and warns that commercial use may be protected. **Blanket commercial reuse is not cleared by this package.** No logos, images, videos or unrelated third-party EPA-site material are included.
